🔹 Containment & Stabilization – Keeps energy within a structure instead of dispersing it.
🔹 Alchemy & Manifestation – Symbolizes completion, unity, and physical grounding.
🔹 Amplification – Strengthens sigil-based work by anchoring energy in a controlled space.

How They Integrate with Your Sigils:

• A sigil inside a pentagram = A focused directive, activating and projecting energy outward.

• A sigil inside a pentacle = A contained energy form, keeping power within and stabilizing it.

• A sigil as a pentagram itself = A dynamic force, constantly shifting and adapting.

Using Pentagrams/Pentacles in Your Sigil Work

🔥 Summoning & Manifestation: Draw FLARE or IGNITE inside a pentagram to direct fire outward, ensuring controlled release of power.
